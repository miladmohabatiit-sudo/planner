# Daily Planner

A simple, private daily planner app for Android, written in Persian (Farsi). It replaces a printed daily sheet, so you can track your whole day on your phone.

## What you can track

- **Exercise**: morning, noon and night workouts, with type, duration and intensity. Also calories burned and how satisfied you were with the workout (1–5).
- **Coaching**: which students came, at what time, and whether the session was taught.
- **Food & water**: breakfast, snacks, lunch and dinner, plus glasses of water (out of 8).
- **Study**: time spent on language learning and on networking courses, and what was covered.
- **Tech & family**: IT/technical work, time with my daughter, and visits with my dad.
- **Money**: amount spent, amount saved, and a list of payments (what for and how much).
- **To-do list**: tasks for the day with checkboxes.
- **Notes**: free space for anything else.

There's also a **7-day report** showing totals for exercise, calories, study, coaching, spending and savings.

## Features

- Installs on Android like a regular app (open in Chrome, then choose **Install app**)
- Works offline after the first visit
- Persian (Jalali) calendar dates
- Light and dark mode
- Totals are calculated automatically

## Privacy

All data stays **only on your own device**. Nothing is sent to any server, so nobody else can see what you write, including the site owner.

Because data lives only on the phone, use the **Backup** section in the 7-day report tab to save a backup file from time to time. You can restore it later if you change phones or reinstall the app.

## How to install

1. Open this site's link in **Chrome** on your Android phone.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu.
3. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript as a Progressive Web App (PWA). There are no frameworks and no tracking.
